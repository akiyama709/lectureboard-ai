# Code of conduct

LectureBoard AI aims to support education across languages, disciplines, abilities, and backgrounds. Participation requires respectful, evidence-based, and constructive communication.

Harassment, discrimination, disclosure of private lecture or student information, deliberate misrepresentation of research, and unsafe handling of credentials or personal data are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that conflict with these principles. Concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner.
